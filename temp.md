You’re right. We overcorrected toward **massive infrastructure businesses with deep moats**.

A product does not need to be impossible to copy. A feature can become a great business when:

- It lives inside software people already use
- The marketplace supplies distribution and trust
- Installation takes minutes
- It solves one painful task exceptionally well
- Billing is automatic
- The price is much lower than the value delivered
- Customer data and workflow make it sticky

Shopify supports public marketplace distribution with native recurring and usage billing on the merchant’s Shopify invoice. Atlassian is offering eligible Forge apps 0% marketplace revenue share on their first $1 million of lifetime Forge revenue. Canva, HubSpot and Google Workspace also operate reviewed marketplaces for third-party apps. ([shopify.dev](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution))
